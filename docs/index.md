## Key Points

### Your Data Security

1. **Local Storage Only**
   - PDF files and annotations stay on your device
   - Stored in the app's private directory
   - Other apps cannot access your library

2. **No Backup or Cloud Sync**
   - We don't upload your sheets
   - We can't access your files
   - Device-level encryption protects your data

3. **What Gets Shared**
   - Only **anonymous, aggregated statistics** (via AdMob on Donate page)
   - Google collects device/interest data for ad targeting
   - We never see your personal information

### How to Use Piccolo Music Reader Legally

**✅ Legal Uses:**
- Store music sheets you own or have permission to use
- Personal practice and learning
- Annotate PDFs for your own reference
- Share sheets with people you've authorized

**❌ Legal Issues to Avoid:**
- Importing copyrighted sheet music without rights
- Copying protected PDFs (DMCA concerns)
- Violating music licensing agreements
- Using others' sheet collections without permission

**📝 Responsibility:**
- You're responsible for imported content
- Ensure you have rights to any sheet music you add
- fsoftt is not liable for copyright infringement by users

## Security & Encryption

### Local Data Protection

- **Device-Level Encryption:** Android encrypts app sandbox on encrypted devices
- **No In-App Encryption:** Files are stored as-is (PDFs remain standard PDF format)
- **Sandbox Isolation:** Only Piccolo Music Reader app can access your library directory

### Server Communications

- **HTTPS/TLS:** All app-to-server communication is encrypted
- **AdMob Secure:** Google AdMob uses HTTPS for ad requests
- **No Unencrypted Transmission:** No personal data sent in plain text

### What We Don't Offer

- Cloud backup (your responsibility)
- Cross-device sync (data stays local)
- End-to-end encryption (data never leaves your device, so not needed)

## FAQ

### Q: Does Piccolo Music Reader store my PDFs on a server?
**A:** No. All PDFs are stored locally on your device only. We never see them.

### Q: Can you recover my deleted sheets?
**A:** No. Once deleted from your device, they're permanently gone. We don't have backups.

### Q: What data does Piccolo Music Reader collect?
**A:** We collect nothing about you personally. Only aggregate, anonymous ad data via Google AdMob on the Donate page.

### Q: How do I delete my data?
**A:** Uninstall Piccolo Music Reader. All local data is automatically deleted.

### Q: Can I disable ads?
**A:** Yes. Settings → "Support with Ads" → Toggle OFF.

### Q: Is Piccolo Music Reader open source?
**A:** Yes. See https://github.com/fsoftt/PiccoloReader

### Q: How is my data protected?
**A:** Your device's filesystem encryption (if enabled), plus app sandbox isolation. Your data never leaves your phone.

### Q: Can I export my sheets?
**A:** Yes. Your sheets are stored as standard PDFs in your device's app folder. You can copy them to external storage.

### Q: Will you ever sell my data?
**A:** No. We don't collect personal data, so there's nothing to sell.

### Q: What about GDPR/CCPA?
**A:** Piccolo Music Reader is compliant. See **Privacy by Region** section above.
