# MuseScore General SoundFont

`src/PiccoloReader/Resources/Raw/piano.sf2` is a modified subset of MuseScore_General.sf2
(version 0.2): 17 General MIDI presets, converted to mono with lower sample rates and
shortened notes by `tools/sf2trim`. Upstream: https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/musescore/soundfont/MuseScore_General/

FluidR3 (original version) by Frank Wen Copyright (c) 2000-02

Mono conversion (FluidR3Mono) by Michael Cowgill Copyright (c) 2014-17

Adaptation for MuseScore_General.sf2 by S. Christian Collins Copyright (c) 2018-19

Temple Blocks instrument provided by Ethan Winer Copyright (c) 2002

Drumline Cymbals provided by Michael Schorsch Copyright (c) 2016

MuseScore_General.sf2 is shared under the MIT license, as was FluidR3Mono and FluidR3 before it.

Fluid was constructed in part from samples found in the public domain that were
edited/cleaned/remixed/programmed by Frank Wen, and largely from his own recordings and
those of the people who helped: Suren M. Seron, Scott Hanan, Steve Aupperle, Chris Gillman,
Alex Taubr, Chris Prola, Andrew Klenk, Winfried Hubbe, Dylan, Tim, Gort, Uros Katic,
Ethan Winer (http://www.ethanwiner.com).
